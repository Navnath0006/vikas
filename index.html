As a dedicated Mechanical Engineer with over 5 years of hands-on experience in precision machining and grinding, I bring expertise in CNC operations, tool resharpening, and quality assurance to every project.

My journey began at UCAL Fuel Systems as an in-plant trainee, where I developed a solid foundation in precision manufacturing. I then honed my skills at Bhavani Industries as a CNC Operator, mastering surface and CNC grinding techniques.

Currently serving as an Assistant Engineer at Musashi Auto Parts India Pvt Ltd, I specialize in CNC precision grinding and the resharpening of shaving, broach, and HOB cutters. My expertise with measurement tools like micrometers, calipers, and gauges ensures the highest standards of precision in every task.
